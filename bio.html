Bio

Ella King is an Assistant Professor of Chemical and Biological Engineering and of Materials Science and Engineering at Northwestern University.

Before joining Northwestern, Ella King was a Simons Junior Fellow at NYU. She received a Ph.D. in physics from Harvard University and B.S. from Stanford University. Ella has been recognized with an “Emerging Soft Matter Excellence Award,” as a speaker in the “Distinguished Young Scholars Seminar,” among the “Rising Stars in Soft and Biological Matter”, with a Certificate of Distinction in Teaching, and with an NSF Graduate Fellowship, among others.
